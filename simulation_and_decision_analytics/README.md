# Simulation and Decision Analytics

This repository contains analytical coursework and operational modeling exercises focused on simulation, process optimization, workflow analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

## Key Areas

- process and system simulation
- operations analytics
- queueing and workflow analysis
- decision optimization
- performance evaluation and resource allocation
- analytical modeling using Python, and Excel Solver

## Analytical Focus

The work emphasizes practical operational problem-solving, quantitative reasoning, and simulation-based decision analysis within business and management systems.

## Academic Context

Materials were developed through graduate-level coursework in operations analytics and simulation modeling, including MSE719 – Operations Analytics in the Master of Management Sciences (MMSc) program at the University of Waterloo.
