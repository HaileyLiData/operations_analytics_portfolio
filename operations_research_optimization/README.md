🌊 Operations Research and Optimization

This repository contains analytical coursework and optimization modeling exercises focused on quantitative decision-making, operational optimization, and constrained system analysis. The work combines mathematical modeling with practical business interpretation to explore how quantitative methods support operational efficiency and structured decision-making.

🧠 Key Areas
linear programming and constrained optimization
simplex method and duality analysis
transportation and assignment modeling
sensitivity and shadow-price interpretation
operational trade-off evaluation
quantitative decision-modeling workflows
📊 Analytical Focus

The work emphasizes optimization thinking, mathematical reasoning, and practical interpretation of operational decision systems within business and management contexts. Particular attention is given to understanding how model assumptions, constraints, and objective functions influence real-world operational outcomes and strategic decisions.

🎓 Academic Context

Materials were developed through graduate-level coursework in operations research and optimization modeling, including:

MSE603 – Principles of Operations Research
Master of Management Sciences (MMSc) Program
University of Waterloo

The repository reflects applied analytical training in optimization techniques, quantitative modeling, and operational decision-support frameworks.
